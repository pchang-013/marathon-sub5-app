Sub-5 Hour Pace & Fueling Guide

To achieve sub-5 hours, required average pace is 7:06/km (4:59:59). Training at 7:00/km provides a 4-minute buffer for hill climbs and water stations.

Workout Type Pace (min/km) Effort / RPE
Easy / Recovery 7:50 – 8:10 RPE 3–4 (Conversational, zone 2)
Marathon Pace (MP) 7:00 RPE 5–6 (Smooth, rhythm-focused)
Tempo / Threshold 6:55 RPE 7 (Comfortably hard)
Long Runs (Steady) 7:35 – 7:45 RPE 4–5 (Controlled aerobic stamina)
Race Day Target 7:00 / km Estimated Finish: 4h 55m

Fueling & Hydration Strategy

  • Practice in training: Starting Week 5, take 1 gel at 8 km; Week 6 take 2 gels.
  • Race day cadence: Consume 1 energy gel every 40–45 mins (~6–7 km) with water, never with sports drinks alone.
  • Electrolytes: Practice taking salt tablets / electrolyte chews on long runs over 20 km (Weeks 7, 9, 10, 12).
